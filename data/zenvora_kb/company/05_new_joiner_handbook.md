## Benefits at a glance

- Group health insurance for you and your family (floater of INR 5 lakh), with an annual health check-up.
- Learning budget of INR 30,000 per employee per financial year.
- 24x7 confidential Employee Assistance Programme.
- Home internet allowance of INR 1,000 per month for hybrid employees.
- 2 paid volunteer days a year ('Zenvora Gives Back').

## Culture and rituals

- **Monthly all-hands:** first Friday of each month.
- **Quarterly town hall** with Q&A from the CEO and leadership team.
- **Hack Week** twice a year (April and October).
- **Friday Learning Hour:** one short talk or demo every Friday afternoon.
- **Anniversary awards** at 5 and 10 years of service.
- **Focus Friday:** no internal meetings before 12:00.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many days a week do I need to come to the office?**  
Three days per week as per your team roster. Tuesday to Thursday are the anchor days. Some groups (new joiners in their first 90 days, people serving notice, trainees and Care Centre shift staff) work from office every working day. *(Source: ZEN-POL-002)*

**I just joined. Can I work from home?**  
Not during your first 90 calendar days. Induction means working from the office on all working days. *(Source: ZEN-POL-002)*

**I resigned. Do I still get remote days?**  
No. Employees serving notice work from office on all working days. *(Source: ZEN-POL-002)*

**How many casual and sick leave days do I get?**  
8 casual leave and 8 sick leave days per financial year. A medical certificate is needed for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. *(Source: ZEN-POL-003)*

**How does Earned Leave work?**  
You earn 1.5 days a month (18 a year) from joining, and can use it after probation. You can carry forward up to a balance of 30 days; anything above is encashed in April. *(Source: ZEN-POL-003)*

**What is the notice period?**  
60 days for bands Z1 to Z3, 90 days for Z4 to Z6, 120 days for Z7 and Z8. During probation it is 30 days. *(Source: ZEN-POL-003)*

**How long is maternity leave?**  
26 weeks for the first two children and 12 weeks from the third child. *(Source: ZEN-POL-003)*

**Can I take a sabbatical?**  
After 5 years of service you can take up to 4 weeks unpaid, with approval from your department head and the Head of People & Culture. *(Source: ZEN-POL-003)*

**What happens if I get a verbal warning?**  
It is Phase 1 of progressive discipline. If the issue continues, Phase 2 is a written warning with a PIP issued within 5 business days. *(Source: ZEN-POL-004)*

**Can I appeal a disciplinary action?**  
Yes. Send your information within 5 business days to the Head of People & Culture. A final appeal goes to the CEO. *(Source: ZEN-POL-004)*

**Which offences skip the warnings?**  
Sexual harassment, theft or fraud, violence, working under the influence, intentional data breaches and illegal acts. *(Source: ZEN-POL-004)*

**Can I use my company email for personal things?**  
No. Company email is for company business only. *(Source: ZEN-POL-005)*

**Can I paste customer data into an AI chatbot?**  
No, unless the tool is on the approved list for that data class. Never paste Confidential or Restricted data into unapproved tools. *(Source: ZEN-POL-005)*

**I clicked a phishing link. What do I do?**  
Report it within 1 hour to security@zenvora.example or in #incidents. You will not be punished for reporting. *(Source: ZEN-POL-006)*

**My laptop was stolen.**  
Report it within 2 hours to the helpdesk and security@zenvora.example. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-ITS-01)*

**Who is on the POSH Internal Committee?**  
Tanvi Deshpande (Presiding Officer), Deepa Venkatesh, Lavanya Murthy and external member Adv. Vasundhara Rao. Email posh@zenvora.example. *(Source: ZEN-POL-008)*

**How long do I have to make a POSH complaint?**  
3 months from the incident, extendable by 3 months. *(Source: ZEN-POL-008)*

**Is there a cab for night shifts?**  
Yes, for any shift ending between 20:00 and 06:00, with GPS tracking and a security escort for women on night routes. *(Source: ZEN-POL-007)*

**How do I report an injury or near miss?**  
Within 24 hours through the EHS form in ZenPeople or ehs@zenvora.example. *(Source: ZEN-POL-007)*

**What is my hotel limit when I travel?**  
It depends on your band and the city tier. For example, Z4 to Z6 may spend INR 7,000 a night in a Tier-1 city and INR 5,500 in a Tier-2 city. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-FIN-01)*

**Who approves my travel claim?**  
Your manager up to INR 25,000, your department head up to INR 1,00,000 and the CFO above that. International trips need the CEO. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-FIN-01)*

**When are appraisals?**  
Half-yearly. H1 FY2026-27 self-reviews are due 16 October 2026 and manager reviews by 30 October. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-HR-01)*

**How many reviewers does my pull request need?**  
One for most services. Two, including a Principal Engineer or Director, for billing, payments and authentication services. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-BKE-01)*

**What must be true before QA signs off a release?**  
Zero open P0 or P1 defects, regression pass rate at least 98 percent, 80 percent coverage on new code, a passing performance test and a clean security scan. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-QAT-01)*

**What are the incident severities?**  
SEV1 is a full outage, data loss or breach (acknowledge in 15 minutes). SEV2 is a major degradation (30 minutes). SEV3 is minor (4 hours). SEV4 is low impact (next business day). *(Source: ZEN-DPL-DCL-01)*

**Who approves a 25 percent discount?**  
The CEO and CFO. The CRO can approve up to 20 percent. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-SAM-01)*

**What are the Care Centre shifts?**  
A: 06:00 to 15:00. B: 13:00 to 22:00. C (night, voluntary): 21:00 to 06:00. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-CSC-01)*

**What are the support SLA targets?**  
P1: 15 minutes first response, 4 hours resolution. P2: 1 hour and 8 hours. P3: 4 hours and 24 hours. P4: 1 business day and 3 business days. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-CSC-01)*

**Who do I contact for IT help?**  
Slack #ask-it or it-helpdesk@zenvora.example. Helpdesk runs 08:00 to 20:00 IST on weekdays. *(Source: ZEN-DPL-ITS-01)*

**Can I do freelance work on weekends?**  
Not without written approval from your department head and the Head of People & Culture, and never for competing work. *(Source: ZEN-POL-001)*

**Do I have to declare a gift from a customer?**  
Yes if it is worth more than INR 3,000, within 7 days of receiving it. *(Source: ZEN-POL-001)*
