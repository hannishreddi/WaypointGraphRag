# Customer Story: Medilink Clinics and Zenvora Care

*A fictional case study written by Marketing (Meghana Prabhu) with input from Sales (Priyanka Choudhury) and Customer Care (Suresh Babu Kothapalli).*

**Customer:** Medilink Clinics, a chain of 23 neighbourhood clinics in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
**Product:** Zenvora Care, using Zenvora Pulse as the shared inbox.
**Went live:** 2 June 2025, as one of the first two brands in Project Horizon.

## The problem
Medilink received appointment, report and billing questions through WhatsApp, phone calls and email. Front-desk staff answered them between patients, so replies took hours. Patients often asked the same question on two channels and got two different answers.

## What Zenvora did
Priyanka Choudhury scoped the engagement with Medilink's operations head and agreed three service levels: first response within 15 minutes for urgent appointment changes, within 1 hour for report queries and within 4 hours for billing. Anand Sagar built a Medilink-specific training module and knowledge base. Lavanya Murthy rostered a dedicated pod of three agents including Kiran Kumar Patnaik as senior specialist. Billing and report-related replies are always written or approved by a human agent, as required by the Support Operations, Shift and SLA Policy.

## Results after 12 months
- About 1,100 tickets a month handled by Zenvora Care.
- Median first response time of 12 minutes.
- CSAT of 92 percent.
- Front-desk staff time spent on messages fell by roughly 60 percent.
