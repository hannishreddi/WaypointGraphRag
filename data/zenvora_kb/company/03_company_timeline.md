# Company Timeline

*Fictional document created for a GraphRAG demonstration project. All people, companies and figures are invented.*

| Date | Type | Event | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Mar 2014 | Founding | Zenvora incorporated in Hyderabad | Arvind Kulkarni and Meera Subramanian leave Kestrel Networks and start Zenvora from a rented room in Madhapur. |
| 12 Oct 2015 | Product | Pulse 1.0 launches | First release: shared inbox for email and SMS, with six pilot customers. |
| 20 Apr 2016 | Funding | Seed round of INR 2.5 crore | Led by Godavari Angels Network. |
| 13 Jun 2016 | Organisation | First Head of People & Culture joins | Lakshmi Prasanna Varma builds Zenvora's HR function. |
| 1 Aug 2017 | Product | WhatsApp Business channel added to Pulse | Becomes the most-used channel within a year. |
| 2 Apr 2018 | Office | Bengaluru Engineering Hub opens | Opened to hire senior backend, ML and SRE engineers. |
| 5 Nov 2018 | Product | Zenvora Care launches | Managed customer care using Pulse as the work tool. First client brand is Swift Courier. |
| 1 Apr 2019 | Organisation | Data & AI team formed | Ananya Bhattacharya joins to start the function. |
| 20 Jan 2020 | Product | Zenvora Ledger general availability | Invoicing and reconciliation for small businesses. |
| 23 Mar 2020 | Organisation | Company moves to fully remote work | Response to the COVID-19 lockdown; Care Centre staff work in split shifts with transport support. |
| 15 Feb 2021 | Office | Visakhapatnam Care Centre opens | Zenvora Care operations move from Hyderabad to Visakhapatnam. |
| 28 Jun 2021 | Funding | Series A of INR 18 crore | Led by Deccan Capital Partners. |

| 3 Oct 2022 | Office | Austin sales presence established | Ashwin Pillai joins as Business Development Manager, US. |
| 1 Apr 2023 | Project | ZenPeople HRMS goes live (Project Compass) | Attendance, leave, payroll and expenses in one system. |
| 10 Nov 2023 | Award | Pulse named SME Tech Product of the Year | Awarded at the Southern India SaaS Summit 2023. |
| 31 Mar 2024 | Project | Monolith decommissioned (Project Atlas complete) | Deploy time down from 3 days to 40 minutes. |

| 30 Sep 2025 | Compliance | SOC 2 Type II report issued | Project Sentinel closes. |
| 19 Dec 2025 | Project | Project Ember closes | Cloud spend down 31 percent. |
