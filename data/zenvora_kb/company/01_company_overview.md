## Mission and vision

**Mission:** Help small and mid-sized businesses talk to their customers and keep their books straight without hiring a big team to do it.

**Vision:** To be the default customer-engagement and finance-automation platform for 50,000 growing businesses in India and the US by 2030.

## Values

- **Own it:** If you see a problem, it is yours until you hand it to someone who has accepted it.
- **Say it plainly:** Short, direct writing beats polished vagueness. Write the memo, state the risk, name the owner.
- **Keep the customer in the room:** Every design review and sprint review starts with a real customer conversation or ticket.
- **Build to last:** We prefer boring, well-tested solutions over clever ones that only one person understands.
- **Grow people on purpose:** Every person has a named manager, a written growth plan and a mentor.

## Products and services

- **Zenvora Pulse** (launched 12 Oct 2015): Omnichannel customer-engagement platform. One shared inbox for WhatsApp, email, SMS, web chat and voice, with automation, tagging and analytics for SMEs.
- **Zenvora Ledger** (launched 20 Jan 2020): Invoicing, GST e-invoicing, payment reconciliation and cash-flow forecasts for small businesses.
- **Zenvora Care** (launched 5 Nov 2018): Managed customer-care service. Zenvora's trained agents answer customers on behalf of client brands, using Pulse as the work tool.
