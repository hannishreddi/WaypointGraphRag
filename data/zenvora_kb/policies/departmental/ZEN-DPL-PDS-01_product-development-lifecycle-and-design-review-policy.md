## 1. Objective

To decide what to build based on customer evidence, and to launch features that Support and Sales are ready for.

## 2. Applies to

Product managers, designers and anyone proposing product changes.

## 3. Stages

1. Discover: customer research and problem definition.
2. Define: written product requirements document (PRD) with success metrics.
3. Design: concept and detailed design reviews with Engineering and QA.
4. Build: feature flags on, QA involved from the first sprint.
5. Launch: launch checklist completed.
6. Learn: review metrics 60 days after launch.

## 4. Customer research

Every product manager holds at least 5 customer interviews per quarter. Customer participation requires consent.

## 5. Launch checklist

- Documentation and help-centre articles ready.
- Support training delivered at least 5 working days before launch.
- Security and accessibility checks passed.
- Marketing brief approved by the Marketing Manager.
- Pricing approved if changed.

## 6. Roadmap and sunsets

The roadmap is reviewed quarterly by the leadership team. Retiring a feature needs 6 months' notice to customers, and ending a beta needs 90 days. Pricing changes need the approval of the CEO, CRO and CFO.
