## 1. Objective

To reimburse reasonable business expenses promptly and consistently, and to keep travel spend within budget.

## 2. Applies to

All employees travelling or spending on behalf of Zenvora.

## 3. Approvals

| Amount per trip or claim | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to INR 25,000 | Reporting manager |
| INR 25,001 to INR 1,00,000 | Department head |
| Above INR 1,00,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Any international trip | CEO (and CFO for budget) |

The CEO's own travel is approved by the CFO.

## 4. Hotel limits per night

| Band | Tier-1 city | Tier-2 city |
|---|---|---|
| Z1 to Z3 | INR 4,500 | INR 3,500 |
| Z4 to Z6 | INR 7,000 | INR 5,500 |
| Z7 and Z8 | INR 11,000 | INR 8,500 |

Tier-1 cities: Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Pune and Kolkata. All other cities are Tier-2.

## 5. Transport

- Flights: economy class. Band Z6 and above may use premium economy on flights longer than 5 hours.
- Train: AC 3-tier for Z1 to Z4, AC 2-tier for Z5 and above.
- Local travel: ride-hailing or auto. Own vehicle: INR 8 per km for a car and INR 3.5 per km for a two-wheeler.

## 6. Daily allowance

Domestic food allowance is INR 1,200 per day for Z1 to Z4 and INR 1,500 per day for Z5 and above. International per diem is USD 75 for Z1 to Z5 and USD 100 for Z6 and above.

## 7. Claims

- Submit within 30 days of the end of the trip in ZenPeople Expenses with receipts for every item above INR 500.
- Finance settles approved claims within 10 working days. Advances up to 80 percent of the estimate are available.
- Client entertainment needs prior approval and is capped at INR 2,500 per head.

## 8. Not reimbursed

- Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment, class upgrades paid by choice, and expenses without receipts above the limit.
- False claims are a serious conduct issue under the Code of Conduct (ZEN-POL-001) and the Progressive Discipline Policy (ZEN-POL-004).

## 9. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
