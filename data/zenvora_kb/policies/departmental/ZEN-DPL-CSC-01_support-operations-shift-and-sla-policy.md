## 1. Objective

To answer customers quickly and consistently, with fair shifts and safe working conditions.

## 2. Applies to

All Customer Support employees and partner BPO agents working on Zenvora queues.

## 3. Shifts and rosters

| Shift | Hours (IST) |
|---|---|
| A (morning) | 06:00 to 15:00 |
| B (general) | 13:00 to 22:00 |
| C (night) | 21:00 to 06:00 |

- The Team Lead publishes the roster 7 days ahead. Everyone gets one weekly off. Shift swaps need Team Lead approval.
- Night shift is voluntary and paid a night allowance of INR 300 per night. Cab transport applies to any shift ending between 20:00 and 06:00 (ZEN-POL-007).
- Support staff work on site. Working from home is allowed only in a declared contingency such as a flood or transport strike.

## 4. SLA targets

| Priority | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (service down) | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 (major issue) | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 (normal) | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| P4 (question) | 1 business day | 3 business days |

## 5. Escalation

1. Specialist works the ticket.
2. Escalate to the Team Lead when 50 percent of the SLA time has passed.
3. Escalate to the Head of Customer Care for any P1 or P2 SLA breach.
4. Engineering escalation goes through the on-call engineer (ZEN-DPL-DCL-01).

## 6. Quality and targets

- CSAT target: 90 percent or above. First-contact resolution target: above 80 percent.
- The Quality & Training Analyst audits 5 contacts per agent per week with a target score of 90 percent.
- Call recordings are kept for 90 days.

## 7. Overflow to partner BPO

When the queue exceeds 25 waiting contacts for more than 10 minutes, overflow goes to partner BPO Sutradhar Business Services. Partner agents see masked customer data only.

## 8. AI assist

During the Sage pilot, agents review every AI-drafted reply before sending. Billing disputes, legal and health or safety topics must always be written or approved by a human (ZEN-DPL-DAI-01). Never take screenshots of customer data or keep personal notes about customers.

## 9. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
