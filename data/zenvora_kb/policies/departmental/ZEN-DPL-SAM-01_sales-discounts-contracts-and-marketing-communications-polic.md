## 1. Objective

To win business honestly, protect margins and keep contracts and communications compliant.

## 2. Applies to

Everyone who quotes, sells, signs or markets Zenvora products.

## 3. Discount approvals

| Discount on list price | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 10 percent | Account executive or business development manager |
| Above 10 and up to 20 percent | Chief Revenue Officer |
| Above 20 percent | CEO and CFO |
| Contract term longer than 3 years | CEO |

## 4. Contract authority

- Annual contract value up to INR 10 lakh: signed by the Chief Revenue Officer.
- Annual contract value above INR 10 lakh: signed by the CEO.
- Non-standard liability, data or exclusivity terms need legal review through the CFO, and security questionnaires need CISO review.
- Payment terms longer than net 45 days need CFO approval.

## 5. CRM discipline

Every opportunity is logged in HubSpot within 1 business day. Forecasts are updated every Monday.

## 6. Outreach rules

India: respect the Do-Not-Disturb registry and give a clear opt-out. US: follow CAN-SPAM, include an unsubscribe link and a physical address in marketing email.

## 7. Marketing claims

- Claims about performance or security must be backed by data or reports (for example ISO 27001 and SOC 2).
- Customer logos and quotes need written consent.
- Press statements are approved by the CEO. Social posts follow the Acceptable Use Policy (ZEN-POL-005).

## 8. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
