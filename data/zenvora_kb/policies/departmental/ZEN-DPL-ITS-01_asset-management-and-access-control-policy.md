## 1. Objective

To keep a trustworthy inventory of devices and to give people the access they need and nothing more.

## 2. Applies to

All employees, contractors and anyone issued a Zenvora device or account.

## 3. Devices

- A laptop is issued on day 1 with accounts ready by 09:30. Laptops are refreshed after 3 years.
- All devices are enrolled in MDM. Only software on the approved list may be installed.
- Report a lost or stolen device within 2 hours to the helpdesk and security@zenvora.example.
- Return devices within 3 working days of leaving. Disposal is certified.

## 4. Access requests

Request access in Jira Service Management. Approval needs the manager and the system owner. Production access is just-in-time. Shared accounts are not allowed, and admin accounts are separate from daily accounts.

## 5. Joiner, mover, leaver

- Joiner: access ready on day 1.
- Mover: access re-checked within 5 working days of a role change.
- Leaver: all access removed within 4 hours of the last working hour (immediately for involuntary exits).

## 6. Access reviews

The CISO runs access reviews every quarter with system owners.

## 7. Helpdesk

| Priority | Target |
|---|---|
| P1 (cannot work) | 30 minutes |
| P2 (workaround exists) | 4 hours |
| P3 (request) | 1 business day |

Helpdesk is staffed 08:00 to 20:00 IST on weekdays. Shift staff in Visakhapatnam reach IT through Slack #ask-it or it-helpdesk@zenvora.example.
