## 1. Objective

To set clear goals, give regular feedback, rate performance fairly and link pay, promotion and development to performance.

## 2. Applies to

All employees confirmed or on probation with at least 3 months in the cycle.

## 3. Cycle

The financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March. There are two half-yearly cycles: H1 (April to September, reviewed in October) and H2 (October to March, reviewed in April). Salary revisions take effect on 1 April, based on the H2 review. Promotions are considered in April, with exceptional off-cycle promotions possible in October.

## 4. Goals

Within 3 weeks of the start of each cycle, every employee agrees 3 to 5 OKRs (objectives and key results) with the manager in ZenPeople. Goals are reviewed at monthly one-to-ones.

## 5. Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Needs improvement |
| 2 | Partially meets expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 5 | Outstanding |

No more than 10 percent of a department may be rated 5 in a cycle.

## 6. Calibration

After manager reviews, the department head and the HR Business Partner calibrate ratings across the department to keep standards consistent. Ratings are shared with employees only after calibration.

## 8. Rating of 1 or 2 and PIP

An employee rated 1, or rated 2 in two consecutive cycles, is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan under Phase 2 of the Progressive Discipline Policy (ZEN-POL-004).

## 9. Special situations

- Employees on maternity or other long leave are rated pro-rata on the period worked and are not penalised for the absence (ZEN-POL-003).
- Employees serving notice are not eligible for increments or promotion.
- New joiners with less than 3 months in the cycle are reviewed in the next cycle.
