## 1. Objective

To explain what is acceptable when using Zenvora's devices, email, chat, internet and approved AI tools, and how the company monitors usage.

## 2. Applies to

All employees, interns, consultants and vendors using Zenvora systems, networks or accounts.

## 3. Company email

- Every employee gets an email address in the form name.surname@zenvora.example. It is for company business only.
- Formal email includes customer agreements, official documents, reports and letters to applicants. Personal email (friends, family, shopping) must not be used for company business.
- Never use a customer's email address for internal communication.
- Access to company email is a privilege and can be restricted without notice if needed to protect the company.

## 4. Chat and collaboration tools

Use Slack for internal chat, Confluence for documentation and Jira for work tracking. Do not move customer data into personal chat apps. Customers are contacted only through Pulse business numbers and company email.

## 5. Internet use

Internet access is provided for work. Brief personal use is fine if it does not affect work or security. Sites with offensive, violent, sexually explicit or illegal content are blocked and must not be accessed on company devices.

## 6. AI tools

- Use only AI tools on the approved list maintained by IT & Security and Data & AI.
- Never paste Confidential or Restricted data (customer conversations, source code of Pulse core, personal data, financials) into any AI tool that is not approved for that data class.
- You are responsible for checking AI-generated code, text and numbers before using them.

## 7. Personal devices

Personal phones may be used for Okta, email and Slack only if enrolled in mobile device management (MDM). Personal laptops are not allowed for company work.

## 8. Social media

Do not speak for Zenvora on social media unless authorised by Marketing. Never post customer information, screenshots of internal tools or unreleased plans.

## 9. Monitoring and audits

Zenvora may monitor and log use of its systems, including sites visited, downloads, email and chat on company accounts, to protect security and comply with law. IT & Security audits systems periodically for abuse or misuse.

## 10. Consequences of misuse

Abuse or misuse of company technology leads to warnings or disciplinary action under the Progressive Discipline Policy (ZEN-POL-004), up to and including termination. Deliberate data theft or access to illegal content may lead to immediate termination.

## 11. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
