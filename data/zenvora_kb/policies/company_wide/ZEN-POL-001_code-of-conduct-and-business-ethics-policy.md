## 1. Objective

To set out how everyone at Zenvora is expected to behave towards customers, colleagues, partners and the company, so that decisions can be made without a rulebook for every case.

## 2. Applies to

All employees (all bands), interns, consultants and contractors working for Zenvora.

## 3. Our values in practice

The five values (Own it, Say it plainly, Keep the customer in the room, Build to last, Grow people on purpose) are the test for any situation this policy does not cover. If in doubt, ask your manager or the HR Business Partner before acting.

## 4. Respectful workplace

- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with courtesy. Bullying, abusive language, discrimination on any ground and retaliation for raising a concern are not tolerated.
- Sexual harassment is handled under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment Policy (ZEN-POL-008) and the Internal Committee, not by line managers.
- Zenvora is an equal-opportunity employer. Hiring, pay, promotion and exits are decided on merit and role requirements.

## 5. Conflict of interest

Declare any situation where a personal interest could affect, or appear to affect, your judgement at work. Declarations go to the Head of People & Culture through ZenPeople.

- Hiring, managing or influencing the pay of a close relative requires written disclosure and a different decision-maker.
- Financial interests above 1 percent in a customer, supplier or competitor must be declared.
- Board seats or advisory roles outside Zenvora need written approval of the department head and the Head of People & Culture.

## 6. Moonlighting and outside work

Employees may not take paid or unpaid work, consulting or freelance projects outside Zenvora unless approved in writing by their department head and the Head of People & Culture.

- Approval is never given for work that competes with Zenvora, uses its confidential information or equipment, or affects availability during working hours.
- Unapproved moonlighting is treated as a serious conduct issue under the Progressive Discipline Policy (ZEN-POL-004).
- Open-source contributions and teaching on personal time are allowed without approval if no confidential material is used.

## 7. Gifts, hospitality and anti-bribery

- Never offer, promise or accept a bribe or improper payment, directly or through a third party, to or from any public official or private party.
- Gifts or hospitality worth more than INR 3,000 per giver per year must be declared to HR within 7 days of receipt. HR may ask you to return or donate the gift.
- Client entertainment follows the limits in the Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy (ZEN-DPL-FIN-01).

## 8. Confidential information and intellectual property

Customer data, source code, pricing, financials and unreleased plans are confidential. Handle them under the Information Security Policy (ZEN-POL-006). Work created in the course of employment belongs to Zenvora. Your confidentiality duty continues after you leave.

## 9. Raising concerns (whistleblower channel)

Report suspected violations to your manager, the HR Business Partner, or confidentially to ethics@zenvora.example. Concerns about the CEO or senior leaders can be sent directly to Dr. Anjali Mehra, independent director and chair of the Audit Committee. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a serious violation.

## 10. Consequences

Breaches of this Code are handled under the Progressive Discipline Policy (ZEN-POL-004). Fraud, bribery, theft and harassment are not subject to progressive steps and may lead to immediate termination.

## 11. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
