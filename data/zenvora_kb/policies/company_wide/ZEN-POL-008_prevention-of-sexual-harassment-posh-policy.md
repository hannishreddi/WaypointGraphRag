## 1. Objective

To prevent sexual harassment and to provide a safe, confidential and fair process to deal with complaints, as required by the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. Zenvora extends the same protection to employees of every gender.

## 2. Applies to

Everyone who works for or with Zenvora, in every location, including interns, consultants, contractors and visitors, and also covering events, travel and online interaction.

## 3. What counts as sexual harassment

- Unwelcome physical contact or advances, requests for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, showing pornography, or any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.
- Conduct in person, by message, email, chat, calls or on social media, at the office, at client sites, on work travel, at events, or on remote days.

## 5. How to complain

1. Submit a written complaint to the Presiding Officer within 3 months of the incident (or the last incident). The IC may extend this by a further 3 months for good reason. The IC will help you put the complaint in writing if needed.
2. The IC may arrange conciliation if the complainant requests it. No monetary settlement is used as the basis of conciliation.
3. If there is an inquiry, the IC completes it within 90 days, gives both parties a fair hearing and submits its report within 10 days of completion.
4. Zenvora acts on the recommendations within 60 days, using the Progressive Discipline Policy.

## 6. Interim relief

During an inquiry the IC may recommend interim measures such as a change of reporting line or seat, or up to 3 months of leave for the complainant.

## 7. Confidentiality and non-retaliation

The identity of the people involved, the complaint and the inquiry are confidential. Anyone who retaliates against a complainant or witness faces disciplinary action. A knowingly false complaint is also a conduct violation.

## 8. Awareness and reporting

All employees complete POSH awareness training within 30 days of joining and annually after that. The IC files an annual report as required by law.
