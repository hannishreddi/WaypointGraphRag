## 1. Objective

To explain the types and amounts of leave, how to apply, and how leave is treated when employment ends.

## 2. Applies to

All full-time employees. Interns receive leave as stated in their offer letter.

## 3. Leave entitlements per financial year (1 April to 31 March)

| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 8 days | Pro-rated in the year of joining. Cannot be carried forward. Maximum 3 consecutive days. |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 8 days | Pro-rated. Medical certificate needed for more than 2 consecutive days. Cannot be carried forward. |
| Earned Leave (EL) | 18 days (1.5 per month) | Accrues from the date of joining and can be availed after probation. Carry forward up to a balance of 30 days. |
| Public holidays | 12 days | Calendar published each January, plus 2 floating holidays chosen from a list of 6. |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks (first two children), 12 weeks (third child onwards) | As per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961. Extended 'work from home' option after leave by agreement with the manager. |
| Paternity leave | 10 working days | To be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. |
| Marriage leave | 5 working days | Once during employment. |
| Bereavement leave | 5 working days | For death of a parent, spouse, child or sibling. |
| Volunteer days | 2 days | Paid days for company-approved volunteering ('Zenvora Gives Back'). |
| Compensatory off | 1 day per holiday or weekly off worked | Must be availed within 60 days. Needs manager approval before the work day. |

## 4. How to apply

- Apply in ZenPeople. For planned leave of more than 2 days, apply at least 3 working days ahead.
- In case of sickness, inform the manager by 10:30 IST on the first day and apply in ZenPeople on return.
- Unapproved absence of 3 or more days in a row is treated as absconding and handled under the Progressive Discipline Policy (ZEN-POL-004).

## 5. Probation and notice period

- New joiners are on probation for 6 months. EL can be availed only after probation is confirmed.
- Notice period after probation is 60 days for bands Z1 to Z3, 90 days for Z4 to Z6, and 120 days for Z7 and Z8. During probation it is 30 days.
- Leave may not be used to shorten a notice period unless the manager and the Head of People & Culture agree in writing. Employees serving notice work from office on all working days (ZEN-POL-002).

## 6. EL encashment and carry forward

At the end of the financial year, EL above a balance of 30 days is encashed with the April payroll. On exit, the remaining EL balance is encashed at basic pay divided by 26 per day. CL and SL are not encashed.

## 7. Sabbatical

Employees with at least 5 years of service may take up to 4 weeks of unpaid sabbatical once, with the approval of the department head and the Head of People & Culture. Benefits continue during the sabbatical.

## 8. Leave during maternity, paternity and long absence

Employees on maternity leave continue to receive group health insurance. Their performance is rated pro-rata and the absence is not held against them in appraisals (see ZEN-DPL-HR-01).

## 9. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
