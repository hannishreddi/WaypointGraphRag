## 1. Objective

To give people flexibility on where they work while keeping enough time together in the office for collaboration, mentoring and customer-facing work.

## 2. Applies to

All full-time employees. Interns follow the on-site rules for Z1. Consultants follow their contract.

## 3. Standard rule: three days in office

- Every employee works from the office (WFO) at least 3 days per week, as set in the team roster.
- Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the anchor days. Teams plan shared meetings, design reviews and demos on these days.
- Monday and Friday are flexible. Remote days are worked from home (WFH) or from a client site.
- Client visits and business travel count as WFO days.

## 4. Who must work from office on all working days

The following groups work from office on every working day, without exemption, until the condition ends:

- Trainees and interns in band Z1.
- New joiners in the first 90 calendar days after the date of joining (induction).
- Employees serving a notice period after resignation or termination.
- Employees not allocated to any project or work stream for more than 10 working days.
- Customer Support employees on the Care Centre shift roster (see ZEN-DPL-CSC-01).

## 5. Rosters and deviations

- The reporting manager designs the team roster and publishes it in ZenPeople at the start of each month.
- Any deviation from the roster must be approved in advance by the department head.
- Employees may work from home 'anywhere in India' for up to 12 days per financial year with department head approval. Working from another country needs written approval from the Head of People & Culture and the CFO because of tax and legal exposure.

## 6. Attendance and working hours

- Normal working time is 9 hours a day. Core hours are 10:30 to 16:30 IST, when everyone should be reachable.
- In office, attendance is recorded by the door access card (first entry and last exit).
- On remote days, employees clock in and out in ZenPeople. Attendance is validated against timesheets and leave records.
- Two unapproved roster deviations in a calendar month lead to a conversation with the manager and HR Business Partner (Phase 1 of ZEN-POL-004).

## 7. Sickness and leave

An employee who falls ill on a WFO or WFH day should apply for Sick Leave under the Leave Policy (ZEN-POL-003) rather than work unwell.

## 8. Working from home standards

- Follow the Code of Conduct (ZEN-POL-001) and dress code norms during video meetings.
- Follow the Acceptable Use Policy (ZEN-POL-005) and Information Security Policy (ZEN-POL-006): use only company laptops, no public Wi-Fi for Restricted data, lock your screen.
- A home internet allowance of INR 1,000 per month is paid to employees in hybrid mode on request through ZenPeople Expenses.

## 9. Meeting etiquette

- Cameras on in team meetings on remote days. Start and end on time.
- Focus Friday: no internal meetings before 12:00 on Fridays unless urgent.
- Share an agenda in the invite. Write down decisions and owners in Confluence or Slack the same day.

## 10. Austin Regional Supplement

Employees based at the Austin sales office are remote-first. They meet customers in person where useful, use the Austin co-working space as needed, and follow the US public holiday calendar. All other parts of this policy and of the Code of Conduct, Acceptable Use and Information Security policies apply, subject to local law.

## 11. Compliance

Regardless of work mode, every employee must follow all Zenvora policies, including the Leave Policy, Code of Conduct, Acceptable Use Policy and Information Security Policy. Failure may lead to action under the Progressive Discipline Policy (ZEN-POL-004).

## 12. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
