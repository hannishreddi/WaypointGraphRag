## 1. Objective

To provide safe and healthy working conditions at every Zenvora location and to reduce the company's environmental footprint.

## 2. Applies to

All employees, interns, consultants, contractors and visitors at Zenvora premises or acting on behalf of Zenvora.

## 3. Our commitments

- Comply with applicable health, safety and environmental laws and standards.
- Identify hazards, reduce risks and learn from incidents and near misses.
- Train employees and involve them in keeping workplaces safe.
- Reduce energy and paper use and promote sustainable practices.

## 4. Emergency readiness

- Fire drills are held twice a year (April and October) at every location.
- Floor wardens are named for each floor. There are certified first-aiders at each location: 4 in Hyderabad, 2 in Bengaluru and 3 in Visakhapatnam.
- Emergency numbers and assembly points are displayed at every entrance.

## 5. Reporting incidents and near misses

Report any injury, incident, near miss or unsafe condition within 24 hours using the EHS form in ZenPeople or ehs@zenvora.example. Reporting a near miss is encouraged and never penalised.

## 6. Night-shift and commute safety

Employees whose shift ends between 20:00 and 06:00 get company cab transport, with GPS-tracked vehicles and a security escort for women employees on night routes. This applies to the Care Centre roster (ZEN-DPL-CSC-01).

## 7. Ergonomics and wellbeing

- A desk and chair assessment is offered to every employee at joining and for hybrid work set-ups on request.
- Take short breaks from screens. An annual health check-up and group health insurance are provided.
- A confidential Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers free counselling 24x7.

## 8. Employee responsibilities

- Follow safe work procedures, signs and instructions.
- Report injuries, incidents and near misses promptly.
- Take part in drills and training.

## 9. Disclaimer

Zenvora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. reserves the right to change or revise this policy at any time, in any lawful manner, with notice to employees through ZenPeople and Slack #announcements.
