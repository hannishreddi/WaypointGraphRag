**Product ID:** PR01 | **Launched:** 12 Oct 2015

## What it is

Omnichannel customer-engagement platform. One shared inbox for WhatsApp, email, SMS, web chat and voice, with automation, tagging and analytics for SMEs.

## Technology

Go, Python (FastAPI), PostgreSQL, Kafka, React, Flutter, AWS EKS

## Scale (FY2025-26)

312 customers, about 2.3 million customer conversations per month (FY2025-26).
