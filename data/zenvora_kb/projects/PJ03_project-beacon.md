# Project Beacon

**Project ID:** PJ03 | **Status:** Completed | **Start:** 1 May 2023 | **End:** 28 Feb 2025

**Executive sponsor:** Meera Subramanian (Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer)  
**Project lead:** Ananya Bhattacharya (Head of Data & AI)  
**Departments involved:** Data & AI, DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure

## Summary

Build the company data platform: an Iceberg lakehouse with Airflow and dbt pipelines feeding analytics and machine learning.

## Goals

- Single source of truth for product and finance data
- Hourly freshness for Tier-1 tables
- PII masking and governed access

## Outcomes

- Over 300 modelled tables in production
- Weekly business report time fell from 6 hours to 25 minutes
- PII masking applied to all conversation tables

## Technology

Apache Iceberg, Trino, Airflow, dbt, S3, Metabase
