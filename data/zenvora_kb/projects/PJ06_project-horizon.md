**Project ID:** PJ06 | **Status:** Completed | **Start:** 6 Jan 2025 | **End:** 31 Jul 2025

**Departments involved:** Customer Support (Zenvora Care), Human Resources (People & Culture), IT & Information Security, Sales & Marketing

## Summary

Expand the Visakhapatnam Care Centre from 20 to 45 seats, with a new training programme and a partner-BPO overflow arrangement.

## Goals

- Support four new client brands
- Hire and train 12 in-house agents plus partner overflow
- Keep CSAT at 90 percent or above during ramp

## Outcomes

- Seats grew from 20 to 45 (including partner seats)
- Four new client brands went live
- CSAT stayed at 91 percent during ramp-up
