# Project Ember

**Project ID:** PJ07 | **Status:** Completed | **Start:** 1 Aug 2025 | **End:** 19 Dec 2025

**Executive sponsor:** Ramesh Iyengar (Chief Financial Officer)  
**Project lead:** Prakash Raman (DevOps & Cloud Lead)  
**Departments involved:** DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure, Finance & Accounts, Backend Engineering

## Summary

Cloud cost optimisation (FinOps): rightsizing, savings plans, storage tiering and per-team cost allocation.

## Goals

- Cut annual AWS spend by 25 percent
- Tag every resource to a team and product
- Monthly cost review with Finance

## Outcomes

- Annual AWS spend fell by 31 percent, about INR 92 lakh a year
- Cost allocation tags on 98 percent of resources
- Monthly FinOps review established

## Technology

AWS Cost Explorer, Terraform, Datadog, Kubernetes (EKS)
