# Project Compass

**Project ID:** PJ02 | **Status:** Completed | **Start:** 1 Oct 2022 | **End:** 30 Apr 2023

**Project lead:** Tanvi Deshpande (HR Business Partner)  
**Departments involved:** Human Resources (People & Culture), Finance & Accounts, IT & Information Security

## Summary

Replace spreadsheets and email approvals with ZenPeople, a single HRMS for attendance, leave, payroll and expenses.

## Goals

- One system of record for employee data
- Automated payroll with statutory filings
- Self-service leave, attendance and expense claims

## Outcomes

- ZenPeople went live on 2023-04-01
- Payroll run time fell from 3 days to 4 hours
- Leave and expense approvals moved fully online

## Technology

ZenPeople HRMS (vendor product), Okta single sign-on, Tally Prime integration
