# Project Sage

**Project ID:** PJ08 | **Status:** In progress (beta) | **Start:** 15 Sep 2025 | **End:** ongoing

**Executive sponsor:** Meera Subramanian (Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer)  
**Project lead:** Ananya Bhattacharya (Head of Data & AI)  
**Departments involved:** Data & AI, Customer Support (Zenvora Care), Product & Design, Quality Assurance, Sales & Marketing

## Summary

Zenvora Sage, a retrieval-augmented AI assistant that drafts and resolves Tier-1 support replies inside Pulse, with human review for sensitive categories.

## Goals

- Deflect at least 35 percent of Tier-1 tickets
- Hallucination rate below 2 percent on the golden set
- General availability by 2026-11-30

## Progress so far

- Beta started 2026-03-02
- Pilot at the Care Centre deflected 38 percent of Tier-1 tickets
- Hallucination rate 1.4 percent on the golden set

## Technology

Python (FastAPI), PyTorch, Vector search (pgvector), Open-weight and hosted LLMs, Airflow
