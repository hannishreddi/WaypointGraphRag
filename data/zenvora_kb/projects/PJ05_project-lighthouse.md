**Project ID:** PJ05 | **Status:** Completed | **Start:** 3 Jun 2024 | **End:** 31 May 2026

**Departments involved:** Frontend & Mobile Engineering, Product & Design, Quality Assurance, Sales & Marketing

## Summary

Rebuild Pulse Mobile as Pulse Mobile 3.0 in Flutter, with a redesigned inbox and the Zenith Design System.

## Goals

- Crash-free sessions above 99.5 percent
- App store rating above 4.5
- Offline-first inbox

## Outcomes

- Crash-free sessions rose from 97.1 to 99.6 percent
- App store rating rose from 3.9 to 4.6
- Task success in usability tests rose from 71 to 93 percent
- Staged rollout completed on 2026-05-05

## Technology

Flutter, Dart, Firebase Crashlytics, Figma, Playwright (web parity tests)
